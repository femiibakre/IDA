About IDA

Empowering Communities Through Financial Support

The International Development Assistance (IDA) program is a philanthropic initiative intended to provide financial support to eligible individuals and families facing financial challenges.

Through targeted assistance, IDA empowers recipients to navigate hardships, pursue lifelong goals, and build sustainable futures for themselves and their communities.

Financial Relief

Direct assistance starting at $25,000 for qualifying applicants

Educational Growth

Funding academic pursuits, tuition, and specialized training

Business Development

Supporting entrepreneurs to scale and create local jobs

Livelihood Improvement

Enhancing quality of life and fostering self-sufficiency

Who Can Qualify?

Inclusive Support for Every Journey

The initiative aims to support people from different walks of life, providing resources to help navigate financial challenges.

Low-Income Families

Families working to overcome economic hardship, manage essential living costs, and establish stable households.

Entrepreneurs

Driven innovators seeking initial capital or resources to establish, sustain, or expand business initiatives.

College Students

Undergraduate and graduate students striving to complete degrees without the burden of overwhelming expenses.

Retirees

Retired individuals on fixed incomes needing supplementary assistance to maintain dignified living standards.

Senior Citizens

Elderly community members requiring financial peace of mind for healthcare, wellness, and everyday living.

Individuals with Disabilities

Individuals facing physical or cognitive challenges, supported with aid to foster independent, fulfilling lives.

Assistance Overview

Potential Financial Assistance

According to the program’s description, qualified applicants may be eligible for financial assistance starting at $25,000, with higher amounts depending on eligibility and the program’s terms.

  • Manage essential expenses: Housing, utilities, medical care, and food security.
  • Pursue education: Tuition fees, certifications, research materials, and equipment.
  • Develop businesses: Seed funding, equipment purchases, inventory, and marketing.
  • Improve livelihoods: Achieving lasting stability, self-reliance, and well-being.

Core Values

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

IDA’s stated mission is to encourage community development, support education, provide emergency assistance, reduce poverty, and help individuals achieve greater financial stability.

Our Vision

The vision behind IDA is to promote compassion, opportunity, and support for people who need assistance. Our stated purpose is to encourage a future where financial difficulties do not prevent individuals and families from pursuing a better life.
